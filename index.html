Attribution2026
Designed and fixed end to end

Google Ads to CRM conversion tracking

Paid leads were booked without the click ID reaching the paid invoice, so Google could not see which ads produced revenue. I traced the causes with COQL and rebuilt the path from click to Closed Won.

  • Captured GCLID, WBRAID, GBRAID and UTM values from the website, forms, chat and DM channels into CRM fields.
  • Built invoiceToDeal: when a direct booking is paid it finds the person, attaches or creates the deal, writes attribution first, then closes it as won. It fixed 9 stuck paid deals.
  • Compared three conversion routes and proved Google's scheduled import works (188 conversions in 30 days) while a Zoho-side push was dead. Recommended switching the dead route off.
